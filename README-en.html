ANGELSAUDIOBOX

Beat'N'Block · User Guide

What is it?

AngelsAudioBox is a drum machine that runs directly in your browser. Load your own sounds, create patterns and perform live. No installation required — just double-click and go.

Getting Started

1
Unzip the file anywhere on your computer.
2
Double-click launch.bat — the app opens automatically in your browser.
3
If the browser asks for permission to play audio, click Allow.
Works with Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Chrome recommended.

Loading Sounds

Each track (KICK, SNARE, HI-HAT...) can play any sound you choose. The samples/ folder contains ready-to-use sounds organized by category.

1
Open the samples/kick/ folder in your file explorer.
2
Drag a .wav or .mp3 file onto the DROP zone of the KICK track in the app.
3
The filename appears on the track — the sound is loaded.

Repeat for each track with the sound of your choice.

Creating a Rhythm

By clicking

Click on the grid cells to toggle steps on or off. You can also click and drag to paint multiple steps at once.

By expression

Each track has a small text field. Type a formula to generate the rhythm automatically.

ExpressionResult
euclidean(4, 16)4 beats evenly spread across 16 steps
euclidean(3, 8)3 beats over 8 steps — natural groove
[1,0,0,1,0,0,1,0]Exact pattern you define yourself
fill(0.5)Random — 50% chance per step
fill(0.75)Dense random — 75% chance per step

Press Enter to apply.

Pattern Memories A B C D E F G H

Each track has 8 independent pattern memories. Click a letter to store or recall a different pattern. Great for having multiple variations ready to switch live.

Sets A and B

The interface has two columns — Set A and Set B. Each set has its own 8 tracks and patterns. The crossfader at the top lets you smoothly transition between them during a performance.

Step Count

The 16 badge on the right of each track is clickable. It toggles between 8 and 16 steps. Mix both across different tracks to create polyrhythmic patterns.

Saving Patterns

At the bottom of the screen, type a name and click SAVE. Your patterns are saved in the browser. To reload: click the name in the list then LOAD.

Starter Ideas

Kick: euclidean(4, 16) — classic 4/4 kick
Snare: [0,0,1,0,0,0,1,0] — snare on beats 3 and 7
Hi-Hat: fill(0.75) — dense, lively hihat
Perc: euclidean(5, 16) — asymmetric groove
